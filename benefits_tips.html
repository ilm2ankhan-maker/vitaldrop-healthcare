Tips for Blood Donors

Before Donation

  • Get enough sleep
  • Eat a healthy meal
  • Drink plenty of water
  • Avoid fatty foods

During Donation

  • Wear comfortable clothing
  • Relax and stay calm
  • Inform staff if you feel unwell

After Donation

  • Rest for 10-15 minutes
  • Have some refreshments
  • Avoid heavy lifting
  • Keep yourself hydrated
